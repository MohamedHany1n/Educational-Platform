# 2.1 Related Systems

This page compares our platform with common solutions used for online teaching.

| System | Type | Strengths | Gap for our target teachers |
|--------|------|-----------|-----------------------------|
| Google Classroom | Classroom tool | Free, easy sharing of material and assignments | No built-in course payment; no parent progress view as a core feature |
| Moodle | Open-source LMS | Very complete, many plugins | Heavy to install and configure; not designed around manual local payment approval |
| Udemy / Coursera | Course marketplaces | Large audience, polished experience | Teacher does not control students and fees; commission and platform rules |
| Chat groups and file sharing | Informal | Zero setup | Unorganized, no grading, no payment tracking |

## How Educational Platform differs
- One simple place for **courses, videos, exams and grading**.
- **Manual payment approval** (InstaPay / Vodafone Cash) matching the local way of paying.
- A **parent role** to follow children's progress.
- An **admin** who controls users, payments and reports.

> This comparison is a general overview based on common knowledge of these tools.
